Vimshottari Dasha

Generate a chart on the "Chart & Analysis" tab first. Computed automatically on every Generate Chart from the Moon's nakshatra at birth — the classical 120-year Vimshottari cycle, shown to 4 levels: Mahadasha (MD), Antardasha (AD), Pratyantardasha (PD) and Sookshma Dasha (SD). Click a planet name in a column to open its sub-periods in the next column. The currently running period at each level is highlighted orange; any row you click is highlighted yellow.

Sade Sati

Generate a chart on the "Chart & Analysis" tab first. Computed by scanning transiting Saturn's real position across ~80 years from birth — every Sade Sati episode (Saturn transiting the 12th/1st/2nd sign from the natal Moon), with dates, ages, house effects, and a good/neutral/bad quality read per phase. This scan can take a few seconds the first time.

Shadbala

Generate a chart on the "Chart & Analysis" tab first. Six-fold classical planetary strength (Sthana/Dig/Kala/Cheshta/Naisargika/Drik Bala) for the 7 classical planets, in Rupa/Virupa units, with a dynamic interpretation of why each planet is strong or weak and what that means — strength is capacity to act, not a verdict on whether the outcome is favorable.

Ashtakvarga Predictions

Generate a chart on the "Chart & Analysis" tab first. Reuses this chart's existing BAV/SAV data (Ashtakvarga tab) — nothing here recomputes bindus. Notation: this app uses the BINDU convention (0-8 per sign), never Rekha. Predictions are background-support indications, not guaranteed events — always read alongside natal promise, dasha and transit.

Kakshya Analysis

Generate a chart on the "Chart & Analysis" tab first. A micro-timing refinement layered on the existing Ashtakavarga data: each sign is divided into 8 Kakshyas of 3°45' each (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Lagna). This never overrides natal promise, dasha, or the larger transit — it only refines when, within an already-indicated transit, the effect is more or less likely to express.

Kakshya Life-Area Timeline

Visual timeline of Kakshya-based support across major areas of life. Generate a chart on the "Chart & Analysis" tab first.

How to read this chart: Green periods indicate stronger calculated support for the selected area of life, yellow indicates mixed or limited support, and red indicates more difficult periods. The colour is based on the combined assessment of the relevant Ashtakavarga (BAV/SAV), Kakshya, and current Dasha factors — it is a supportive/challenging timing assessment, not a guarantee that any event will or won't happen. Kakshya is a refinement/timing layer; it should not be read independently of the natal chart and dasha.

Remedies_1

Generate a chart on the "Chart & Analysis" tab first. Chart-specific remedies (Chant, Donation, Action, Gemstone) drawn from a fixed, curated database — never improvised at runtime. Every remedy shows why it was chosen, from which chart factors, with its source and confidence level. No remedy here is a guarantee of any outcome — this is a traditional astrological practice guide, not medical, legal, or financial advice.

Remedies_2

Personalized Parashari Remedy Analysis — a second, independently-built deterministic remedy engine (separate from Remedies_1) evaluating natural/functional nature, house, sign, dignity, lordship, conjunctions, aspects, combustion, debilitation/cancellation, Shadbala, yogas and dasha activation for all 9 planets. Traditional remedial practice guidance only — not a medical, legal, or financial guarantee.

Remedies_Compare

Side-by-side comparison of Remedies_1 and Remedies_2 — two independently-built deterministic remedy engines evaluating the same chart. Both reuse the same underlying dignity/functional/Shadbala/dasha/yoga calculations but score, consolidate and rank independently, so agreement between them is a useful cross-check, and disagreement highlights where the two rule sets weigh factors differently — not that one is "wrong."

Knowledge Base

A supplementary Parashari interpretation reference (Lagna-in-sign, Planet-in-house) for the current chart, drawn from a standard/consensus interpretive synthesis of common Parashari principles — not a verbatim quotation of any single classical text. Planet-in-sign content is a stub in this knowledge base (structure present, text not yet authored) and is shown honestly as such rather than invented.

Nakshatra Interpretations

For each planet, an interpretation of its current Nakshatra (lunar mansion) and Pada (quarter), synthesized from this app's existing classical reference data — each nakshatra's deity/symbol/keynote, its Vimshottari star lord, the planet's own core significations, and the pada's Navamsa-sign flavor (using this app's existing D9 divisional chart calculation, since one pada is by definition exactly one Navamsa division). Every statement traces back to one of these reused sources — nothing here is freely invented per planet/nakshatra combination.

Sudarshana Chakra

A three-reference-point analysis of the SAME natal chart — Lagna (external life), Moon (mind/experience), and Sun (identity/status/vitality). The planets never move between these views; only the house numbering (relative to each reference sign) changes. This is a confirmatory/analytical framework, not a replacement for the complete Parashari system — natal promise (from Lagna) remains the foundation, and dasha/transit activation are always considered alongside the three-reference synthesis.
